# Grade 7 — final year of CABA primaria

33 lessons in the 11 packages G07-01–G07-11 from `lesson-creation-plan.md`.
Both es-AR and en-US describe the same CABA-oriented editorial course, ages 12–13.
This is not a claim of verified formal curriculum alignment.

- 66 lesson checks and 55 fresh package checks: 121 bilingual questions.
- Package checks: recognition, two procedures, application, reasoning. The shared
  review threshold is 4/5 including application and reasoning; this is an editorial
  hypothesis, not a governmental criterion.
- Every lesson includes an objective, prerequisite warmup, worked situation, three
  explanatory steps, visual cards, misconception, and open exit task.
- A final fair-design project includes a worked rubric for adult review.
- Six new interactive models: shared-stick patterns, formula/table/graph,
  intersecting angles, area/perimeter scaling, cube layers and paired faces,
  and progressive revelation of a fixed recorded coin sequence.
- Reuses Grade 6 direct-proportion and mean models. All controls support keyboard
  input and provide textual results; the models do not submit answers to a server.

G07-05 (formulas) and G07-10 (chance) are explicitly additional explorations because
the plan leaves their regional placement unverified. They are not prerequisites of
the final project and nothing is locked behind them. Formula use remains concrete;
chance separates observed frequencies, outcomes, and model assumptions. The coin
sequence is fixed classroom data, not random simulation. The box diagram shows
layers and paired faces, not a folding net; net construction is a paper activity.

Open explanations use adult review; automatic checks only evaluate selected answers.
Practice does not yet save lesson mastery or grant game rewards. Suggested revisits
after 7–14 days are guidance, not an implemented scheduling system.
